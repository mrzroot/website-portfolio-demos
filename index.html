About

I like problems where a small, well-made tool removes hours of repetitive work.

Most of my time goes into Python automation, backend APIs and the internal dashboards that teams actually live in — SMS operations, budgeting workflows, inventory and request tracking. Many of them are Persian, right-to-left business apps, so I care about getting RTL, dates and data entry right.

On the side I publish open-source utilities: browser extensions, print bridges, CI examples and tooling for AI coding agents — and I maintain a curated resource list for Persian-speaking developers.

Local printing bridge for POS & thermal printers

A tray agent that lets web apps print PDFs and TSPL thermal labels silently through a localhost API — no browser print dialog. Forked from AnouarSbia/printbridge for POS and label workflows.
