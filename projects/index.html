Three small tools for finding evidence, inspecting data, and testing workflow inputs. Try them with synthetic samples or your own text.

01 / LANGUAGE

Persian Retrieval Lab

آزمایشگاه بازیابی متن فارسی

Paste passages and rank the best matching evidence using local BM25. This demo retrieves text; it does not generate an answer.

Inspect the shape of a CSV and spot missing values, duplicate values, and mixed column types. Comma-separated, with a header row.
